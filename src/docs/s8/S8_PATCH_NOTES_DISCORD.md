# 🚀 SEASON 8 — Practice Range & Fairness Update

Season 8 is live! Here's everything that's changed.

## 🎯 NEW: Practice Range

A brand new no-stakes testing area — try any character, any sector, any difficulty, any starting build. **No rewards, no leaderboard, no kill credit** — pure practice.

- **Test locked content** — every character, sector, and difficulty is unlocked
- **Pick your starting level** — jump straight to Lv 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30
- **Any starting weapon** — including weapons you haven't unlocked yet
- **In-run dev tools** (wrench icon on the right side of the screen):
  - Spawn any enemy (including bosses) in any quantity
  - Grant any weapon or passive on demand
  - Force level-up whenever you want
  - Toggle invincibility & infinite cooldowns
  - Fast-forward time 1× / 2× / 4×
  - Clear all enemies with one click
  - Heal to full HP

> Practice runs are completely isolated — nothing you do here touches your save, leaderboard, or economy.

Enter from the Hub → **"PRACTICE RANGE"** tile.

## 💀 REVIVE ESCALATION

Weekly revive costs now **escalate** to prevent whales from buying infinite second chances:

- First few revives: standard price
- Each additional revive this week: **higher cost**
- Cap: **15 paid revives per ISO week**

Resets every Monday 00:00 UTC.
