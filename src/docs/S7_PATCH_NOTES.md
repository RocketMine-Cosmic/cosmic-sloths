# Season 7 — "Defenders Rise" Patch Notes

**Live: Mon Jun 15 2026, 00:00 UTC** (week 25 rollover)

The S7 update is all about **build variety at the top end**. S6 leaderboards were dominated by shield-stacking, and tank-armor builds were unplayable past S15. We're nerfing the shield meta, but more importantly we're **making defensive builds actually work** — armor and HP now scale meaningfully into Outer Galaxy, and three new score levers reward aggressive play across every difficulty.

This is a balance-only patch — no save wipe, no relic reset beyond the normal season prestige. All your characters, talents, gold, NFTs and cosmetics carry over.

---

## 🛡 Shield rework — defense, not domination

The "perma-fortress" shield-bubble strat is going away. Shield is still strong as a defensive layer, but you'll need a real DPS weapon alongside it.

- **Cooldown floor lifted on Shield Bubble, Aegis Matrix, and Burning Barrier.** Minimum cooldown raised from 0.5× to 0.85× of the weapon's base — you can no longer stack 6-8 overlapping shields. Expect 2-3 overlapping at max CDR instead.
- **Pushback decay.** Shields now lose their pushback force in the final 25% of their lifetime, creating natural "press-in" moments where enemies close the gap. Plan your movement around the shield rhythm.
- **Base damage trimmed.**
  - Shield Bubble: 15 → **12** (-20%)
  - Burning Barrier: 18 → **15** (-17%)
  - Aegis Matrix: 40 → **28** (-30%)
- **What this means:** Aegis Matrix is still the strongest pushback option (it's evolved — you earned it), but it's now in the same DPS tier as Quantum Collapse and Buzzsaw Swarm instead of 5× above. Players who built around shields will want to add a high-DPS offensive weapon to keep clearing late sectors.

---

## 🪖 Armor and HP — tanks are viable again

This is the change we're most excited about. Pre-S7, tank builds died in 2 hits at S20 regardless of investment. Now:

- **Armor is % damage reduction**, scaled per sector.
  - Inner Galaxy (S1-S10): up to 20-25% damage reduction
  - Outer Galaxy (S11-S20): up to 30-35% damage reduction
  - 1 armor = 1% reduction (capped by the sector cap)
- **Max HP ceiling scales with sector.** The 2000 HP cap now lifts gradually through Outer Galaxy — players reaching S20 can hit ~4600 max HP with full investment.
- **What this means:** Pandypaws with maxed HP + armor talents goes from "dies in 2 hits at S20" to "survives 8-9 hits at S20." Tank, defensive-relic and regen builds are now real alternatives to evasion or shield strats.

---

## 💥 Nukes — herd thinner, not screen wiper

- **Nuke damage 10× max HP → 2.5× max HP.** Still one-shots Inner Galaxy mobs. T13-T14 Outer Galaxy mobs take ~40%.
- **Nuke drop rate halved.** Luck builds still see more nukes than non-luck builds, just less often.
- **What this means:** Nukes become a "clear the chaff" tool instead of a "delete the screen, then AFK" tool. Picking up a nuke is still hype, but you can't lean on them as your primary clear.

---

## 🌌 Outer Galaxy HP curve — flattened

The S20 HP wall was effectively impassable for non-shield builds (~9M HP per mob at end-of-sector Cosmic). We've redrawn the curve so 5+ build archetypes are all viable at S20.

- New end-of-sector multipliers: S11 = 2×, S15 = 6×, S20 = **11×** (down from 698×)
- Real S20 mob HP: ~143k (was ~9M)
- TTK at median DPS (~12k DPS): ~12s per S20 mob
- Bosses scaled separately via the existing `× 0.3` boss factor — Pulsar Guardian at S20 ≈ 109k HP, 2-3 min fight at median DPS

**What this means:** Sectors S11-S20 actually feel like a progression now, not a brick wall. Builds that don't use shields can clear S20 without nuke spam.

---

## 🔥 HEAT — push the difficulty, get rewarded

A new score multiplier rewards aggressive play across every difficulty.

- **Dynamic Difficulty now ramps on Normal and Hard** (was Cosmic-only). Spawn rates and enemy speed climb as you push your kill rate.
  - Normal: up to 1.75× spawn rate
  - Hard: up to 2.5× spawn rate
  - Cosmic: unchanged at 3.5×
- **HEAT score bonus.** Your final score is multiplied by **up to 2.0×** based on how high you pushed Dynamic Difficulty during your run. AFK runs stay at 1.0× HEAT; aggressive runs hit 2.0×.
- HUD shows "HEAT 2.4×" when DD passes 1.5× so you can chase the cap in real time.

**What this means:** Same effective score ceiling as before for top-tier players who already push DD, but a clear *visible* reward for the aggressive playstyle. Aggressive Normal/Hard players who couldn't access DD's score bonus now can.

## ⚙ Quality-of-life

- Character signature triggers (banner damage, scrap, decoys, hacks, sonic boom, chain explosions) are all tagged in the post-run weapon breakdown. You can finally see exactly which part of your build did the work.

## What's NOT changing

- **No character rebalancing.** Character kits and stats stay as-is.
- **No talent/mastery/relic rule changes.** Bias allocation, forge augments, relic prestige all unchanged.
- **No Inner Galaxy difficulty changes.** S1-S10 are exactly as you remember them.
- **Cosmic DD unchanged.** Existing top-tier players see no DD nerf — the 3.5× cap stays.

---

## Why this patch

S6 had two clear meta-build problems:
1. **Shield-AFK dominated leaderboards.** Lowest skill floor, highest ceiling. Builds B/C (sniper crit, AoE synergy) existed but were strictly harder for similar reward.
2. **Tank/armor builds were dead.** Flat armor was rounding noise once mobs dealt 700+ damage at S20. Pandypaws and defensive talent paths felt like trap options.

S7 doesn't kill the shield — it caps the meta. And it makes the *other* defensive identity (heavy armor + HP) genuinely competitive for the first time.

If Build A2 (e.g. dense pool weapons + banner camp) emerges as the new AFK meta in 2-3 weeks, we'll address it in a mid-season patch. But the goal is to see at least 5 distinct build archetypes near the top of the S7 leaderboard.
