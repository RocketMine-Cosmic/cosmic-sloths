# 🦥 COSMIC SLOTHS — QUICK GUIDE 🌌

## 💰 CURRENCIES

**Gold** — main meta currency, from runs & dailies
**Star Fragments** — augments & passives (Forge converts gold, 30 batches/day)
**Relic Fragments** — craft & prestige relics
**$OMENX** — real token: payouts, revives, boosters, cosmetics

## 📈 UPGRADES

Stats, weapons and talents come in **Permanent / Weekly / Seasonal**. Stacking weekly+seasonal is **cut by 50%** — permanent is the best investment.

**Relics:** 5 exist, **equip 2**. Level 1→5, then prestige.

## 💀 OMENX SPENDS (in-run)

**Revive** — one per run, priced by how deep you died: **4 / 8 / 15 / 25 OMENX** (0–4min / 4–8 / 8–11 / 11min+ or any Endless death). Price always shown first.
🎲 Reroll 2 · 🚫 Banish 2/4/6 · ⚡ XP Buff 10 · 🛡️ Squad Ult 5 or 10

**Fragment Express** — Star Fragments for OMENX, skipping the daily cap: 15 frags / 10 OMENX, or 150 / 100.

💡 Every OMENX spent grows the prize pools.

## 🏆 PAYOUTS

Pools are funded by a **% of all OMENX spent in the game**:
- 💠 **Weekly Score — 30%** → Top 20
- 🔶 **Weekly Kills — 30%** → Top 20 by **sector** kills (endless/raid/meteor don't count)
- 💜 **Seasonal Score — 10%** → Top 20 over the 4-week season
- 👑 **Squad Champions — 10% of the seasonal pool** → Top 3 squads (needs 2+ wars & 2+ members)

#1 takes the biggest share, then flatter bands down to #20. Scores are recomputed server-side — cheats get blacklisted.

## 🛡️ SQUADS

Weekly **Squad Wars** (most kills wins) · **Treasury** buffs everyone donates gold for · **Squad Meteor** · **Daily Goals**. Joining a squad is free power.

## ✨ COSMETICS

**Buyable now (Armoury, gold or OMENX):** Trails, Kill Effects, Skins. Every pilot also has a free seasonal skin.

**Not buyable:** Leaderboard Frames, Animated Pilot Icons and Title Flairs are **chest drops or quest rewards only** — they show "Coming soon" in the Wardrobe. Titles and Jukebox tracks unlock through play.
