Evening all — no new content, just a pass on how the game looks and runs. Most of this you reported.

**🔥 The screen mash**
The glow behind every projectile was drawn at 3× size and stacked additively — hence the white-out at high level. Now a third the area at half the brightness, and projectiles lost the white core that ate their colour.

**⚔️ Mastery and evolutions were invisible — that one's on me**
• **Ricochet Blade**'s promised Silver Blade never rendered.
• **Shield Bubble**'s mastered gold matched SynthBeats' colour exactly — on SynthBeats you couldn't tell. **Aegis Matrix** was that gold too.
• **Hellfire** looked like slightly-more-Napalm. Violet-blue now.
• **Seismic Whip** flashed magenta then fired cyan, like two weapons.
• Neo Blaster, Nap Beam and Vine Whip used *your character's colour*, so shots blended into your sloth.

**💀 Killing you invisibly**
• **Boss AoE attacks were never drawn.** Void Bomb, Plasma Nova and Meteor Shower count down a blast zone then hit you with nothing on screen. Ground markers now — **so bosses are easier, on purpose.**
• **Hazard rings were smaller than the damage.** You could stand outside the circle and still get hit.
• **Plasma Swarm's main damage had no visual at all.**

**🌡️ Overheating, and Quantum Collapse / Toxic Emitter "losing DPS"**
Not graphics. **The game wrote your whole save to disk on every single kill.** Quantum Collapse can kill 30 enemies in one pulse — 30 full saves in one frame. It stalled, so your damage looked like it vanished. It didn't; the game froze mid-swing. That's what cooked your battery too. Kill counts still save, just on a sane schedule.

**🎯 Also**
• Memory leak that could crash a run — fixed
• Elite kills actually shake the screen now
• 💰 markers no longer cover 💀 markers

Thanks to everyone who flagged the overheating and the weak-feeling weapons — I'd assumed both were graphics and looked in the wrong place.
